Washfolio is a year canvas for people who want a year at a glance, not a chatty journal. Home is the folio: one cell per local day. Empty days stay blank.

• Tap today's marked cell to open the twelve-spoke well and paint a stroke
• Twelve named tones: Gall, Soot, Umber, Ochre, Rust, Rubric, Lake, Indigo, Slate, Moss, Pearl, Ivory
• Neighbor-bleed wash: if yesterday has a stroke, today's ink leans toward it. An isolated day stays pure
• Folio sets the Bleed amount and lists Twelve tones. Restore twelve tones returns the default inks
• Wash lists Neighbor-bleed wash, Quiet days, and wash-run lengths. A gap ends a run
• Folio also has Contact Washfolio, Re-run onboarding, and Reset all data
• Onboarding pages: Paint the year, Tap today, Watch it bleed, A wash, not a streak
• There is no tab bar and no entry list. Strokes stay on this device

The year is the home, not a list of notes.
